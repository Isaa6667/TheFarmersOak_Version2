Community rules

Keep The Farmers Oak a friendly place for everyone.

  1. Be kind. No bullying, harassment, hate speech or discrimination.
  2. Keep it friendly. No swearing, spam or inappropriate content, in game or on Discord.
  3. Follow Roblox rules. The Roblox Terms of Use and Community Standards always apply.
  4. No exploiting or cheating. Don’t use scripts, glitches or exploits.
  5. Respect staff. Listen to staff instructions. If you disagree, raise it politely in Discord or by email.
  6. Roleplay fairly. Stay in character where it’s a roleplay event, and don’t ruin other people’s fun.
  7. Stay safe. Never share personal information such as your address, school or passwords.
  8. Free for everyone. Staff will never ask for payment or account details.

Breaking the rules may lead to a warning, a mute or a ban at staff discretion.
