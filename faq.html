Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Is it free to join?

Yes. The Farmers Oak is completely free to join and play.

How do I book a table or event?

Email TheFarmersOak@outlook.com or use the form on the contact page.

Where do I find out about events?

See the events page and our Discord announcements channel.

Can I host a private party?

Yes. Contact us with your date, group size and what you’re planning, and we’ll help set it up.

How do I become staff?

Check the team page for how to apply.

What are the rules?

You’ll find them on the rules page.

Something went wrong. Who do I tell?

Message a staff member on Discord or email us at TheFarmersOak@outlook.com.
