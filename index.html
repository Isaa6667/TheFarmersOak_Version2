Welcome to The Farmers Oak

The Farmers Oak is a traditional country pub where everyone is welcome, whether you’re popping in for a pint after work, meeting friends for a long lunch or settling in for the evening. We pour a carefully chosen range of real ales, serve honest home-cooked food and keep the atmosphere relaxed and friendly.

Come as you are, with family, friends or on your own. We take pride in good service, a comfortable pub and a proper welcome every time. If you’d like to book a table or plan a celebration, just send us an email.

What we offer

Food

Honest, home-cooked pub food made with care, from hearty classics to seasonal specials. View the menu

Ales & drinks

A well-kept selection of real ales, lagers, wines, spirits and soft drinks, so there’s something for everyone.

Parties & private hire

Planning a birthday, family gathering or party? We’d love to host it. Email us with your date and numbers and we’ll help you plan the details.
