Welcome to The Farmers Oak

The Farmers Oak is a traditional country pub where everyone is welcome, whether you’re popping in for a pint after work, meeting friends for a long lunch or settling in for the evening. We pour a carefully chosen range of real ales, serve honest home-cooked food and keep the atmosphere relaxed and friendly.

Come as you are, with family, friends or on your own. We take pride in good service, a comfortable pub and a proper welcome every time.
