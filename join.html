How to join

Get into The Farmers Oak in a few quick steps. It’s free to join and free to enjoy.

  1. Join our Roblox community

    Hit the button below, then join the group so you get notified when we’re open.

  2. Hop into the Discord

    This is where we post announcements, event reminders and chat with regulars.

  3. Read the rules

    Just a few simple rules to keep the pub friendly. Read them here.

  4. Check what’s on

    Have a look at the events page and come along.

  5. Follow us

    Catch clips and news on TikTok, or find every link on our Linktree.
